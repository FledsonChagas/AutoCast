# Rubric: Test Quality

## 5
Tests cover expected behavior, edge cases, regression risks, and failure modes at the right level.

## 4
Tests cover main behavior and important risks with small non-blocking gaps.

## 3
Verification exists but is narrow or mostly smoke-level.

## 2
Tests are weak, brittle, missing important assertions, or do not run reliably.

## 1
No meaningful verification.

## Minimum Approval

Standard route requires 3 or higher. Critical route requires 4 or higher.

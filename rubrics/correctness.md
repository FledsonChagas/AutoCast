# Rubric: Correctness

## 5
Fully satisfies the task brief, acceptance criteria, edge cases, and expected behavior with no evident regressions.

## 4
Satisfies the main objective with minor non-blocking gaps or low-risk edge cases.

## 3
Partially satisfies the objective but leaves important ambiguity or weak edge-case handling.

## 2
Implementation is fragile, incomplete, or likely to regress existing behavior.

## 1
Does not solve the stated problem or breaks important behavior.

## Minimum Approval

Standard route requires 4 or higher.

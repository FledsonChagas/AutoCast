# Rubric: Security

## 5
No evident security regressions. Secure defaults, least privilege, input validation, secrets hygiene, dependency safety, and logging safety are addressed.

## 4
No blocking risk. Minor residual risks are documented and acceptable.

## 3
Some security review occurred, but relevant controls or evidence are incomplete.

## 2
Likely security weakness or missing review for a sensitive change.

## 1
High or critical risk, secret exposure, unsafe permission widening, or direct violation of secure development rules.

## Minimum Approval

All routes require 4 or higher when security-sensitive. Critical route requires 5 or explicit human approval.

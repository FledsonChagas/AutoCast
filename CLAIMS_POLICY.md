# AutoCast Claims Policy

AutoCast should be honest about what has been proven.

## Allowed Claims

Allowed:

- AutoCast defines a framework standard and methodology.
- AutoCast includes official adapters for OpenCode, Claude Code, and Cursor.
- AutoCast is secure by default.
- AutoCast includes self-authored calibration evals.
- AutoCast includes realistic sample case studies.

## Restricted Claims

Restricted unless backed by evidence:

- AutoCast improves delivery quality by a specific percentage.
- AutoCast is enterprise-proven.
- AutoCast is independently validated.
- AutoCast prevents security vulnerabilities.
- AutoCast is certified against a compliance standard.

## Required Evidence Labels

Use labels from `ADOPTION_EVIDENCE.md`:

- `sample`
- `pilot`
- `benchmark`
- `independent`

## Rule

When in doubt, underclaim and publish the raw evidence.
